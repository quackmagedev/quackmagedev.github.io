💡 Ideas

🌌 Massive

  • 📖 Epic: A sprawling 2D sandbox where every biome, creature, and item is pulled from the pages of classic storybooks. Defeat the final boss to "Start a New Chapter," a permanent world transformation that reshapes the environment, enemies, and magic tiers.
  • ⚒️ Smithy: An "action cozy" life-sim RPG where you trade farming for the roar of the forge. Venture out to mine ores and raid dungeons for materials, then master skill-based smithing minigames to craft legendary gear for the kingdom.

🔥 Large

  • 🎨 Nick the Shape Man: A puzzle-platformer where you morph between shapes, each with distinct physics and abilities, to restore color to a world gone gray. Fast, expressive, and physics-driven. GitHub →
  • 🕯️ Clandestine: A narrative mystery inside the claustrophobic stone walls of a 16th-century monastery, where a humble monk unravels theological conspiracy and hushed murders through eavesdropping and lockpicking. Historically authentic and heavily researched. Anyone could be a heretic, or a killer.
  • 🌲 Inside the Hollow: A forest dungeon crawler set in a whimsical-but-uncanny hidden valley, with a full attribute system, class tree, and metal-infused soundtrack. Its saccharine surface slowly erodes, revealing that its creatures are each incomplete beneath the facade.

🔮 Medium

  • 📦 BOXED: A physics-based adventure where you're a sentient cardboard package with no arms, desperately trying to reach your delivery destination. Intentionally unwieldy controls and a destructible world make every inch of progress a comedic battle.
  • 🐸 Frogs with Jobs: A 2D platformer where you launch your frog body across hazardous workplaces using a precision slingshot, chaining leaps with a sticky tongue that grabs crane cables, ceiling fans, and kitchen rafters. Workplace satire, chaotic physics, and guaranteed HR violations.
  • 🧪 Saladbox: An open-ended physics and chemistry sandbox where you mix water, fire, acid, gases, and custom reactive materials to build machines, trigger disasters, or just watch particles collide. Relaxing, wildly complex, no objectives required. GitHub →
  • ⚡ Mega Man X spiritual successor: a tight modern action-platformer in the Mega Man X mold.
