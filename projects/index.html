  • 🎨 Nick the Shape Man: A puzzle-platformer where you morph between shapes, each with distinct physics and abilities, to restore color to a world gone gray. Fast, expressive, and physics-driven. GitHub →
  • 🕯️ Clandestine: A narrative mystery inside the claustrophobic stone walls of a 16th-century monastery, where a humble monk unravels theological conspiracy and hushed murders through eavesdropping and lockpicking. Historically authentic and heavily researched. Anyone could be a heretic, or a killer.
  • 🌲 Inside the Hollow: A forest dungeon crawler set in a whimsical-but-uncanny hidden valley, with a full attribute system, class tree, and metal-infused soundtrack. Its saccharine surface slowly erodes, revealing that its creatures are each incomplete beneath the facade.
