## ✅ Completed Games

### Monkeyin' Around

A frantic co-op party game built around momentum and coordination. Players swing and sling monkeys using independent left/right grab mechanics — you can't do it alone, and doing it together is barely-controlled chaos.

**Godot 4.6 · C# · Jolt Physics**

---

### Heart Platformer

A complete side-scrolling platformer: multiple levels, heart-based health, pixel-art visuals, slopes, spikes, and gamepad support.

**Godot 4.0**

---

### Roshambo

Pick a rule system — Rock-Paper-Scissors, Wu Xing (五行), or Classical Planets — then watch territories fight for dominance. Supports grid, hex, Voronoi, and world map modes with zoom, pan, and mutation.

**Python · Pygame · NumPy**
